Quick Answers

Frequently Asked

How long does delivery take? +

Standard delivery takes 2–5 business days within Sri Lanka. Express delivery (1–2 days) is available at checkout for an additional fee. Digital downloads are instant after payment confirmation.

Can I return or refund a game? +

Physical games can be returned within 7 days if the seal is unbroken. Digital purchases are non-refundable once the key has been revealed. Defective physical items are replaced free of charge — contact us with a photo of the defect.

How do I track my order? +

Once your order is dispatched you will receive a tracking number via email and SMS. You can paste that number into the courier's website to see real-time updates. Orders typically ship within 24 hours of payment.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, bank transfer, and cash on delivery (COD) for eligible areas. Cryptocurrency payments are coming soon.

My game key doesn't work — what do I do? +

Email orders@rockstarnexus.com with your order ID and a screenshot of the error message. We will verify and issue a replacement key or full refund within 2 business hours during working days.
